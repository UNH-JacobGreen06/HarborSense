Initial Reflection

What do you think community-engaged learning is? Please do some research online as a group and then respond.

It's a way to connect the classroom to real-world opportunities, to help solve real-world problems that people everywhere face. After researching as a group, we also found that it's different from just volunteering — the academic work and the community benefit go hand in hand, and reflection is a key part of the process. For us, that means applying our web development skills to an environmental problem that actually matters to coastal communities.

Have you ever participated in any type of community service? If yes, in what capacity?

Yes, I have participated as part of a clean-up crew following natural disasters such as tornadoes and large storms in my home state. Seeing how quickly communities can come together in those situations was a reminder of how much a small, organized effort can accomplish — and that experience is part of what motivates me about this project.

How do you feel about working on a community-engagement project? (i.e. such as designing and developing a website to better the community)

I love it! I enjoy working with people and helping to solve a real problem. It helps me engage with the challenges of society and take a step in the right direction to help solve common problems we all face every day. Knowing the website has a genuine purpose beyond a grade makes the work feel more meaningful and worth doing well.

In which ways can people use the engineering design process as a tool to solve problems and improve life?

In a lot of ways. One example I read about was a student who developed a way to catch trash in the ocean so it wouldn't float around, and another person who developed a method to convert plastic into fuel. Creating drinkable water from saltwater is another great example. What all of these have in common is that someone identified a problem and kept refining their solution until it actually worked — which is exactly the process we are trying to follow with HarborSense.

What information do I need?

I need a better understanding of the problems people face daily, and a broad enough base of knowledge to help create a solution. For this project specifically, that means learning about how ocean pollution works, what sensors can realistically measure, and what coastal communities actually need from a monitoring system. I think starting small is the way to go, and upgrading it slowly based on real feedback would be extremely helpful.
