Initial Reflection

What do you think community-engaged learning is? Please do some research online as a group and then respond.

It’s a way to connect the classroom to real-world opportunities, to help solve real-world problems that people everywhere face.

Have you ever participated in any type of community service? If yes, in what Capacity?

Yes I have participated, as a clean-up crew in natural disasters such as tornadoes and large storms in my home state.

How do you feel about working on a community-engagement project? (i.e. such as designing and developing a website to better the community)

I love it, I enjoy working with people, and helping to solve a problem! It helps me engage in the problems of society, and take a step in the right direction to help solve common problems that we all face every day.

In which ways can people use the engineering design process as a tool to solve problems and improve life?

In a lot of ways, I think a huge one I read about a while ago was this student developed a way to catch trash in the ocean so it wouldn’t float around, and then I saw another person who developed gasoline from plastic. Other examples would be creating drinkable water from salt water.

What information do I need?

I need a better understanding of the problems people face daily, and a broad understanding to help create a solution. I think starting small is the way to go, and upgrading it slowly would be extremely helpful.
