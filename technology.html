Overview

HarborSense uses solar-powered IoT buoy systems to continuously monitor water quality in marinas, ports, and coastal environments. Each unit collects real-time environmental data and transmits it wirelessly to a central dashboard for analysis and alerts.

The goal is to provide early detection of pollution events, especially those caused by storm runoff and sudden environmental changes.

System Architecture

Stage Function
1. Sensing Buoy sensors measure pH, turbidity, temperature, conductivity, and depth
2. Power System Solar panel charges a LiFePO4 battery for continuous operation
3. Data Processing Microcontroller filters and prepares sensor data
4. Transmission LoRa module sends data to a central server over long distances
5. Analysis System detects pollution spikes and environmental anomalies
6. Alerts Notifications are sent when dangerous conditions are detected

Sensor Components

The HarborSense unit is built from durable, marine-grade components designed for long-term deployment in harsh coastal environments.

Component Purpose
Industrial pH Probe Measures acidity and alkalinity of water
Turbidity Sensor Detects water clarity and sediment levels
Conductivity Probe Measures salt and chemical concentration
Depth / Pressure Sensor Tracks water depth and pressure changes
Solar Power Kit (10W) Provides renewable energy for continuous operation
LiFePO4 Battery (12V 10Ah) Stores energy for nighttime and low-light operation
LoRa Communication Module Enables long-range wireless data transmission
Microcontroller & PCB Processes and manages sensor data
Marine Enclosure Protects electronics from saltwater and weather damage

Why This Design Works

This system is designed to be low-cost, scalable, and easy to deploy. Unlike traditional monitoring stations that are expensive and fixed in place, HarborSense buoys can be deployed in multiple locations and expanded over time.

This makes it especially useful for smaller communities, marinas, and coastal regions that currently lack real-time environmental monitoring tools.
