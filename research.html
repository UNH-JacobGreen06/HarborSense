What is the Purpose of the Site?

The purpose of the HarborSense website is to present an innovative solution for monitoring ocean pollution using smart sensor technology. The site is designed to inform users about environmental challenges and demonstrate how real-time data can help communities better understand and respond to pollution.

What Do We Want the Website to Accomplish?

The website aims to raise awareness about ocean pollution, educate users about its causes and effects, and promote HarborSense as a practical solution. It also encourages engagement by providing interactive elements such as maps, data, and surveys.

Who is the Intended Audience?

The intended audience includes marina operators, coastal communities, environmental organizations, researchers, and the general public. These groups can benefit from accessible, real-time environmental monitoring and data-driven insights.

What Problem is Being Addressed?

Many coastal areas lack affordable and accessible tools for monitoring water quality in real time. As a result, pollution often goes undetected until it causes significant environmental damage. HarborSense addresses this issue by providing continuous monitoring and early alerts.

What Type of Content is Included?

The website includes educational information about ocean pollution, real-time data visualizations, interactive maps, technical explanations of the system, market analysis, and user surveys. These elements help inform users while also encouraging engagement and feedback.

Similar or Related Websites

Several organizations provide environmental monitoring and data:

  • NOAA – Provides large-scale ocean and atmospheric data used for research and forecasting.
  • EPA – Offers environmental protection resources, regulations, and water quality data.
  • Oceana – Focuses on ocean conservation and advocacy efforts worldwide.

While these websites provide valuable information, they often lack localized, real-time monitoring tools designed specifically for small communities and marinas. HarborSense aims to complement these resources by focusing on accessibility and community-level impact.
