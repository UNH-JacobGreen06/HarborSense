Purpose

The purpose of this website is to present HarborSense, a system designed to monitor water pollution in coastal environments using smart sensors.

Goals

The website aims to raise awareness, provide information, and demonstrate how technology can help protect marine ecosystems.

Audience

Marina operators, coastal communities, environmental organizations, and the general public.

Problem

Many coastal areas lack real-time monitoring, allowing pollution to go undetected.

Similar Websites

NOAA and EPA provide environmental data, but lack localized, real-time marina monitoring.
