What is the Purpose of the Site?

The purpose of the HarborSense website is to present an innovative solution for monitoring ocean pollution using smart sensor technology. The site is designed to inform users about environmental challenges and demonstrate how real-time data can help communities better understand and respond to pollution.

What Do We Want the Website to Accomplish?

The website aims to raise awareness about ocean pollution, educate users about its causes and effects, and promote HarborSense as a practical solution. It also encourages engagement by providing interactive elements such as maps, data visualizations, and a community survey. Ultimately, the goal is to inspire action and support for cleaner coastal environments.

Who Is the Target Audience?

The primary audience includes coastal community members, marina operators, environmental advocates, students, and researchers who are interested in ocean health and pollution monitoring. The site is written to be accessible to a general audience while still being informative enough for technically minded visitors.

What Problem Does It Solve?

Many coastal areas lack affordable, real-time tools to monitor water quality. Pollution from runoff, storms, and marine traffic often goes undetected until significant damage has already occurred. HarborSense addresses this gap by providing a low-cost, sensor-based monitoring system that gives communities early warnings and actionable data before environmental damage becomes severe.

Similar Websites

During our research we examined several existing platforms that address ocean and water quality monitoring:

  • NOAA – Large-scale environmental data
  • EPA – Environmental protection and water quality data
  • Oceana – Ocean conservation and advocacy

While these websites provide valuable information, they often lack localized, real-time monitoring tools designed specifically for small communities and marinas. HarborSense focuses on accessibility and real-time decision-making.

Key Findings

  • Most participants are very concerned about pollution, especially those living near coastal areas.
  • The majority of users are interested in HarborSense and would use the system.
  • Real-time alerts and visual dashboards are the most requested features.
  • Cost is a significant concern — most respondents prefer pricing under $100 or in the $100–$500 range.
  • 11 out of 12 participants said they want a monitoring system like HarborSense deployed in their area.
  • Pollution and safety were the top two main concerns across all user types.
