Existing Solutions (Competitors)

Several organizations and technologies already monitor ocean and water quality. However, most focus on large-scale data collection rather than local, real-time monitoring.

Solution Type Strengths Limitations
NOAA Monitoring Systems Government Highly accurate, large-scale data Limited local access, not real-time for communities
Satellite Data Platforms Research Global coverage, long-term trends Low resolution at local level
Private Sensor Companies Commercial Advanced technology High cost, limited accessibility
Lir Labs AI Startup AI-powered optical sensors that identify particle type and contamination source in real time; won the 2025 Hult Prize Environmental Sustainability Challenge Designed primarily for on-site water treatment facilities and pipes — not suited for open coastal and marina environments; early-stage startup with limited deployment scale

Market Gap

Current solutions either focus on global-scale monitoring or require expensive infrastructure. There is a clear need for a system that provides affordable, real-time monitoring at the local level.

Phase Description

Why HarborSense is Innovative

HarborSense combines real-time monitoring, affordability, and accessibility into a single system. Unlike existing solutions, it focuses on empowering local communities with actionable data, allowing faster responses to pollution events.
