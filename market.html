Target Market & Customer Segments

HarborSense serves communities and organizations that depend on healthy coastal waters but currently lack affordable, real-time monitoring tools.

SegmentWho They ArePrimary Need
Marina Operators Small to mid-size marina owners and port managers Liability protection and early warning before pollution events escalate
Coastal Communities Municipal governments and local environmental agencies Affordable, automated monitoring without dedicated research staff
Environmental Organizations Nonprofits and conservation groups (e.g., Oceana, local land trusts) Real-time data to support advocacy and policy work
Academic Researchers University marine science programs Affordable field sensors for longitudinal coastal water studies
Commercial Fisheries Fishing operations in coastal and estuarine waters Early detection of contamination events to protect catch quality and safety

Prioritized Risk Assessment

The most significant risks are high initial cost and technical reliability, as both directly impact adoption and community trust. These are prioritized because they carry the highest combined probability and impact scores. Addressing them early — through phased rollout, grant funding, and rigorous testing — increases the likelihood of long-term success.

Why HarborSense is Innovative

HarborSense combines real-time monitoring, affordability, and community accessibility into a single integrated system. Unlike existing solutions that are either too expensive for small communities or too broad for local use, HarborSense is purpose-built for the marina and coastal environment — delivering actionable data to the people who need it most, when they need it most.
