The Problem

Many marinas lack the tools to monitor pollution effectively. As a result, harmful substances such as oil, plastics, and chemicals accumulate over time, damaging ecosystems and threatening marine life.

Our Mission

Our goal is to bridge this gap by making environmental monitoring accessible, affordable, and easy to understand for communities around the world.
