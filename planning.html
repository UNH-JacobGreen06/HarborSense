Website Goal

The goal of the HarborSense website is to inform users about ocean pollution and demonstrate how a real-time monitoring system can help communities detect and respond to pollution more effectively. The site also aims to present HarborSense as a practical and innovative solution for coastal environments.

Expected Results

After visiting the website, users should:

Outcome Description
Awareness Understand the causes and impacts of ocean pollution
Engagement Explore data, maps, and research presented on the site
Interest Recognize the value of HarborSense as a solution
Action Be encouraged to support or learn more about environmental monitoring
